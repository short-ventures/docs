---
title: Connecting Google Analytics
description: Set up GA4 and Google Tag Manager with your landing pages
---

Blox has built-in analytics, but you can also connect Google Analytics 4 (GA4) or Google Tag Manager (GTM) to track your landing pages alongside your other properties.

## Adding your GA4 Measurement ID

1. Open your brand settings
2. Navigate to the **Tracking** section
3. Enter your GA4 Measurement ID (format: `G-XXXXXXXXXX`)
4. Save your settings

### Finding your Measurement ID

In Google Analytics:

1. Go to **Admin** (gear icon)
2. Select your property
3. Click **Data Streams**
4. Select your web stream
5. Copy the **Measurement ID**

## Adding your GTM Container ID

If you use Google Tag Manager:

1. Open your brand settings
2. Navigate to the **Tracking** section
3. Enter your GTM Container ID (format: `GTM-XXXXXXX`)
4. Save your settings

### Finding your Container ID

In Google Tag Manager:

1. Open your container
2. Click the container ID in the header (starts with GTM-)
3. Copy the ID
