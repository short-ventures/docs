Blox includes built-in analytics that track visitors, sessions, and engagement on your landing pages. View these metrics in your page's analytics panel.

## Dashboard overview

The analytics dashboard shows key metrics for your page:

- **Live sessions**: Visitors currently on your page
- **Page views**: Total page loads
- **Unique visitors**: Distinct people who visited
- **Bounce rate**: Percentage of visitors who left without interacting
- **Interaction rate**: Percentage of visitors who clicked, scrolled, or engaged

## Visitor tracking

Blox tracks visitors using first-party cookies:

- **Visitor ID**: Persistent identifier (1-year duration)
- **Session ID**: Identifies a single browsing session
- **Variant**: Which page variant the visitor saw
- **Experiment**: Which experiment they're part of (if any)

This enables accurate counting of unique visitors and return visits.

## Engagement metrics

Beyond page views, Blox tracks how visitors interact:

### Clicks

Tracked on links, buttons, and elements with `data-track` attributes. Each click records:

- What was clicked (element type, text, URL)
- When it happened
- Which variant the visitor was viewing

### Scroll depth

Blox records how far visitors scroll:

- 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% milestones
- Used for scroll-based conversion goals
- Indicates content engagement

### Time on page

Measured when visitors leave or navigate away:

- Total time spent viewing the page
- Whether they interacted during that time
- Used for time-based conversion goals

## Breakdowns

The dashboard includes breakdowns by:

### Device type

- Desktop
- Tablet
- Mobile

Based on screen width to categorize visitors by device.

### Geography

Top countries where your visitors are located, using Cloudflare's geolocation data.

### Traffic sources

Where visitors came from:

- Direct traffic
- Search engines
- Social media
- Referral sites
- Campaign links (UTM parameters)

## Attribution

Blox captures UTM parameters and other tracking identifiers:

- `utm_source`
- `utm_medium`
- `utm_campaign`
- `utm_term`
- `utm_content`
- `gclid` (Google Ads)
- `fbclid` (Facebook)
- `ref` (generic referral)

These appear in attribution reports and are associated with conversions.

## Variant analytics

When you have multiple variants, see metrics broken down by variant:

- Page views per variant
- Unique visitors per variant
- Bounce rate per variant
- Conversion rate per variant

This helps you understand performance differences even without a formal experiment.

## Time series

View metrics over time with the timeseries chart:

- Daily or hourly granularity
- Compare different date ranges
- Identify traffic patterns and anomalies

Use the date picker to select your analysis period.

## Real-time updates

Analytics update continuously as new data arrives. The dashboard shows:

- Current live sessions
- Recent visitor activity
- Up-to-date totals

Refresh the page or check back to see the latest numbers.

## Data retention

Analytics data is retained for the lifetime of your account. Historical data remains available for:

- Long-term trend analysis
- Comparing current vs. past performance
- Audit and compliance purposes

## Export

Currently, analytics data is available through the dashboard. For integrations or external analysis, use:

- Google Analytics connection for additional export options
- Form data export for submission-level details
- API access (contact support for enterprise needs)

## Privacy

Blox analytics are first-party and privacy-focused:

- No cross-site tracking
- Data stays in your account
- Visitor IDs are pseudonymous (not tied to personal identity)
- Compliant with common privacy frameworks when used appropriately

You remain responsible for having appropriate privacy policies and consent mechanisms.
