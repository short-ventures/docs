---
title: Capturing Form Data
description: Collect and manage form submissions from your landing pages
---

Blox automatically captures form submissions from your landing pages. When visitors fill out forms, their responses are stored and available for review, export, and integration with your other tools.

## How form capture works

When a visitor submits a form on your landing page:

1. Blox detects the form submission
2. Form field values are collected and labeled
3. Data is sent to your Blox 'Analytics' panel

## What gets captured

For each form submission, Blox records:

- **Form name**: Identified from the form's ID, name attribute, or nearby headings
- **Field values**: All visible input fields with their labels and values
- **Visitor information**: Visitor ID, session ID, and variant
- **Context**: Country, timestamp, and referrer

## Viewing submissions

To view form submissions:

1. Open your page in the editor
2. Navigate to the **Analytics** panel
3. Select the **Forms** tab
4. Choose a form to see its submissions

Each submission shows all captured fields with their values, plus metadata like timestamp and visitor ID.

## Exporting data

Export your form submissions as CSV:

1. Open the form submissions view
2. Click **Export**
3. Download the CSV file

The export includes all fields and metadata for the selected submissions.

## Deleting submissions

To remove individual submissions:

1. Locate the submission in the list
2. Click the delete button
3. Confirm the deletion

Deleted submissions are permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

## Form submission goals

You can create conversion goals based on form submissions:

1. Go to your brand's goal settings
2. Create a new behavioral goal
3. Select "Form submission" as the event type
4. Use this goal in experiments to measure form conversion rates

This connects form submissions to your A/B testing and analytics.

## Privacy considerations

Form data may contain personal information. Blox:

- Stores data securely in your account
- Limits field value sizes to prevent abuse
- Excludes hidden fields (except Blox metadata)

You're responsible for:

- Complying with privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
- Having appropriate consent mechanisms on your forms
- Deleting data when requested by visitors
