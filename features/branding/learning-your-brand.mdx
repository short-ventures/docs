# Learning Your Brand

When you create a new brand in Blox, our AI automatically analyzes your website to extract your visual identity. This includes your colors, fonts, logos, and even UI components—giving Blox everything it needs to generate landing pages that look and feel like your brand.

## Creating a new brand

To get started, you'll need to provide two pieces of information:

1. **Brand name** — The name of your brand or company
2. **Domain** — Your website URL (e.g., `acme.com` or `https://www.acme.com`)

Once you submit this information, Blox begins analyzing your website automatically.

## The learning process

After you create a brand, you'll see a training progress screen that walks you through six steps:

1. **Initializing** — Setting up your brand workspace
2. **Analyzing website** — Scanning your domain for branding elements
3. **Extracting brand colors and fonts** — Identifying your color palette and typography
4. **Downloading brand assets** — Saving your logos, images, and other visual assets
5. **Creating component library** — Capturing UI sections from your site for reference
6. **Finalizing setup** — Completing the brand profile

This process typically completes within a couple of minutes. You'll see real-time progress updates as each step finishes.

<Note>
If any step fails, you can retry it directly from the training screen without starting over.
</Note>

## What Blox extracts

During the learning process, Blox captures several key elements from your website:

### Colors

Your brand's color palette is automatically extracted. This includes primary colors, accent colors, and any other colors prominently used on your site.

### Fonts

Blox identifies the typography used on your website, including font families, weights, and styles.

### Images

Your logo, favicon, and Open Graph image are downloaded and stored. Blox also captures other images from your site that can be used in your landing pages.

### UI components

Blox takes screenshots of different sections on your website—headers, hero sections, feature blocks, and more. These serve as visual references when generating new landing pages, helping the AI understand your design patterns.

## Refining your brand

After the initial learning process completes, you can manually adjust any of the extracted information. Open the branding settings to access five sections:

### Identity

Update your brand name, description, and website URL. The description helps Blox understand what your company does.

### Colors

Add, remove, or modify your brand colors. Each color can have a tag (like "primary" or "accent") to help organize your palette.

### Images

Upload or replace your logo, favicon, and Open Graph image. These are used across your landing pages and for social sharing previews.

### Fonts

View and manage the fonts associated with your brand. You can add custom fonts if the automatically extracted ones don't match your preferences.

### Rules

Define brand guidelines that Blox should follow when generating content. For example, you might specify tone of voice preferences or words to avoid.

## How your brand is applied

Once Blox has learned your brand, this information is automatically applied when you create landing pages. The AI uses your:

- **Colors** to style buttons, backgrounds, text, and accents
- **Fonts** for headings, body text, and other typography
- **Logo and images** as assets available in your pages
- **UI components** as visual references for layout and design patterns
- **Rules** to guide content generation and maintain brand consistency

This means every landing page you create starts with your brand identity already in place—no manual styling required.

## Updating your brand later

Your brand isn't locked in after the initial setup. You can update any aspect of your brand at any time through the branding settings. Changes apply to new pages you create going forward.

<Tip>
If your website undergoes a major redesign, you can re-run the learning process to capture your updated branding.
</Tip>
