<Update label="v1.2.0">
  **External Goal Tracking**

  You can now track conversions that happen outside your landing pages. Connect your CRM, payment processor, or any external system to attribute conversions back to your Blox experiments.

  - Added external tracking pixel support
  - New API endpoint for server-side conversion tracking
  - Goal attribution now works across sessions
</Update>

<Update label="v1.1.0">
  **A/B Testing is Here**

  Run experiments on your landing pages to find what converts best.

  - Create unlimited variants per page
  - Automatic traffic splitting
  - Statistical significance calculator
  - Winner detection and auto-promotion

  **Form Data Capture**

  All form submissions are now automatically captured and stored in your dashboard.
</Update>

<Update label="v1.0.0">
  **Blox is Live**

  We're excited to announce the public launch of Blox!

  - AI-powered landing page generation
  - Brand learning from your existing assets
  - One-click publishing to custom domains
  - Built-in analytics dashboard
</Update>
