<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much does Blox cost?">
    We have a range of plans to pick from based on your needs. Check out our pricing section to find a plan that works for you. You can cancel or change plan at any time, plus, we provide a 30% discount for annual billing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does the agent train itself?">
    Blox has a proprietary training algorithm - it typically takes under 5 minutes for our engine to train a new agent on your brand. This includes an understand of your visual style, how you write and what your offering is.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I host the landing pages?">
    When your landing page is ready to go - Just click 'Publish' in the top-right of the editor. Your pages will be run through our optimization flow, then published to either your Blox or custom domain.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I manage multiple brands?">
    Absolutely. You can train new agents for new brands and switch between them. From the dashboard, click the brand menu and select 'New Workspace'.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to be technical or understand code?">
    Nope! While your agent knows everything about coding beautiful, high-converting landing pages, all you need to know is how to convey your ideas into words.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I track external conversions?">
    Blox provides a Javascript library which can be used on any website to track external conversions. 
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I connect Google Analytics?">
    In the editor, head to Settings \> Tracking to connect both Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager. For custom tracking scripts, you can use our custom code feature.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do you have a free trial?">
    Yes - Blox provides a 7 day free trial. No risk, cancel at any time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I invite my team?">
    Sure! You can invite your team to Blox on the Scale plan. Go ahead and click on Settings \> Teammates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can you optimize my landing pages for me?">
    Our team knows how to get the most out of Blox. If you want to be one of the first brands to launch autonomous landing pages - get in touch with us at [hello@withblox.com](mailto:hello@withblox.com)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
