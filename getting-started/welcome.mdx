## Blox helps marketers win in the AI era

Coding has always been a bottleneck for landing pages. Marketers want to experiment, but their options are waiting on developers or wrestling with drag-and-drop builders designed in 2005.

Blox changes this.

Our agents train on your brand and can build full landing pages, based on your criteria, in minutes.

We believe landing pages of the future will be autonomous—constantly shape-shifting and learning to create unique, hyper-relevant experiences for every visitor. We work every day to make this a reality.

## What can you do with Blox?

**Build landing pages fast**  
Go from concept to fully-functional page in under a minute. Your agent understands your product, your voice and your customers—positioning it to create pages that convert.

**Experiment with variants**  
Turn a hypothesis into code with one message. Our editor creates variants side by side and lets you launch A/B tests in a click with built-in experimentation.

**Add pop-ups and interactivity**  
You'll never use a traditional pop-up builder again. Turn your vision into gamified widgets, interactive forms and exit-intent pop-ups. Whatever you can imagine, Blox can build.

**Capture leads effortlessly**  
Every form your agent creates is instantly connected to a database. No setup, no integrations—just leads appearing as they submit. Collect any type of data, in any format, and export whenever you need.

**Deploy to your domain**  
Route your landing pages through a subdomain and start using them across campaigns immediately. All traffic flows through our global CDN, loading pages quicker than you can blink.
