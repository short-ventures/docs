## What can you do with Blox?

**Craft creative landing pages**\
Go from concept to fully-functional landing page in just a minute. Your agent understands your product or service, how you write and who your customers are. This uniquely positions it to create high-converting landing pages.

**Experiment with variants**\
Turn a hypothesis into code with one message. Our editor canvas creates variants side by side and enables you to launch A/B tests in a click with our built-in exerimentation.

**Add pop-ups and interactivity**\
You'll never use a traditional pop-up builder again. Convert your vision into gamified widgets, interactive forms and exit-intent pop-ups. Whatever you can think of, Blox can build.

**Capture any kind of data with forms**\
Any form your agent creates is automatically linked to a database, where you can overview and export your leads as they come in. Collect any type of data, in any kind of format.

**Deploy to your domain**\
Easily route your landing pages through a subdomain, so that you can start using your landing pages across your marketing campaigns. All traffic gets routed through our global CDN, loading pages quicker than you can blink.
